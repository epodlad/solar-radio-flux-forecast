# Scientific lineage

Solar Radio Flux Forecast is independent research software by Olena Podladchikova (2026), continuing her earlier short-term forecasting research at ROB/SIDC-STCE. It is not an official institutional service.

Evidence reviewed:

1. Supplied ESWW15 poster: **Real-time short-term forecast of the F10.7 solar index at SIDC**, Olena Podladchikova, Christophe Marque, David Berghmans, Koen Stegen, SIDC forecasting team (author order as displayed on this poster). Describes the SASFF service, random walk, adaptive drift/variance, three daily scheduled runs, 3-day predictions and uncertainties. This supports what the poster states; it is not a server log.
2. Supplied ESWW15 poster: **Adaptive Kalman Filter Based on the Linear Regression for Daily F10.7 Index Forecast**, O. Podladchikova, R. Van der Linden, T. Podladchikova, A. Veronig. Distinct coefficient-state formulation.
3. Bibliographic record pending primary-source verification: **The Self-Adjusted Solar Flux Forecasting Tool (SASFF)**, ESWW 2018, Olena Podladchikova, Christophe Marque, Koen Stegen, David Berghmans and SIDC forecasting team. Preserve this record's own order; do not silently substitute the poster order.
4. Bibliographic record pending verification: **Statistical approaches for the forecast of the F10.7 index**, Olena Podladchikova and Christophe Marque, ESWW 2018.

Later bibliographic record pending verification: **Comparison of Different Approaches for the Real Time Forecast of the F10.7 index at the Space Weather Forecasting Centres**, EGU 2019, Olena Podladchikova, Christophe Marque, Tatyana Podladchikova, Astrid Veronig, Koen Stegen.
